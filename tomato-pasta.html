Tomato Basil Pasta

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh basil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Boil the spaghetti until tender.
  2. Cook the garlic in oil for one minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer for ten minutes.
  4. Toss with the pasta and basil.
