Classic Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 300 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Method

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder and sugar.
  2. Beat the egg, milk and butter, then stir into the flour.
  3. Pour a small ladle of batter into a hot, oiled pan.
  4. Flip when bubbles appear and cook one more minute.
