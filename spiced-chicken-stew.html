Slow-Cooked Spiced Chicken and Vegetable Stew with Coconut Rice

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thighs
  • 2 onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 piece of ginger
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 red pepper
  • 200 g green peas
  • 400 g tinned tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 500 ml chicken stock
  • 300 g rice
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown the chicken in a large pot and set aside.
  2. Cook the onions, garlic and ginger until soft.
  3. Add the spices, tomatoes, stock and chicken; simmer one hour.
  4. Add the vegetables and cook 45 more minutes.
  5. Cook the rice in the coconut milk and serve with the stew.
